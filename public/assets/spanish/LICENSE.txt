Español 1 pictures and Ellen's portrait are original drawings made for this app.
Color cards are flat color shapes, not borrowed artwork.

Spanish speech is bundled in three swappable sets so a voice can be chosen:
- espeak: eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0), voice es-419
- dalia: Microsoft Edge neural voice es-MX-DaliaNeural, generated for preview
- elvira: Microsoft Edge neural voice es-ES-ElviraNeural, generated for preview
Japanese encouragement lines use Microsoft Edge neural voice ja-JP-NanamiNeural.
The neural files are samples for choosing a voice. They are not a Microsoft font or SDK.
